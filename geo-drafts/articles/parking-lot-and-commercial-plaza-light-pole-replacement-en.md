## 1. Jobsite Logistics & Access Constraints

In contemporary infrastructure development, deploying heavy 16-ton mobile boom trucks into constrained spaces—such as narrow historic alleys, unpaved solar farms, or pedestrian corridors—introduces excessive logistical overhead and administrative delays.

Under **OSHA 1926 Subpart CC (29 CFR 1926.1408)**, swinging metallic booms near live overhead electrical utilities mandates minimum clearance buffers of 10 to 20 feet (3.05m to 6.1m). Ground-based compact hydraulic lifting keeps operations entirely at the foundation level, complying with **IEEE 516** clear-distance limits and significantly mitigating electrocution risks.

## 3. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Step-by-Step Two-Person Workflow

### Phase 1: Foundation Alignment & Outrigger Leveling
The two technicians transport the sub-assemblies from a standard utility pickup directly to the foundation. The baseplate is positioned over the embedded anchor studs, and the four leveling outriggers are adjusted using the integrated dual-axis spirit level until level deviation is within ±0.2°.

### Phase 2: Power Unit Coupling & Vertical Hydraulic Erection Stroke
The hydraulic quick couplers are locked and visually verified. The operator activates the 24V hydraulic power unit via the tethered control pendant. The vertical cylinder extends smoothly at approximately 20 mm/s, raising the column to vertical alignment in roughly 20 seconds.

### Phase 3: Mechanical Positive Lock & Torque Verification
Once vertical plumbness is established, mechanical safety stop pins are engaged into the hardened detents to prevent descent. Anchor nuts are torqued in a cross pattern to manufacturer specifications per ASTM F1554 anchor guidelines.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How many workers are strictly required to operate this equipment?
The equipment is strictly engineered for a standard two-person crew. Modular division (<45kg per module) complies with occupational safety standards for manual positioning without heavy cranes.

### What is the maximum rated payload and pole height capacity?
The machine has a strictly verified vertical rated payload of 300 kg, verified by proof-testing on factory vertical test rigs. It accommodates standard street light poles up to a maximum height of 12 meters.

### How does the baseplate accommodate irregular bolt spacing?
The universal baseplate incorporates laser-cut radial slots accommodating pitch circle diameters (P.C.D.) between 180 mm and 260 mm, supporting M16 to M24 anchor studs without requiring field cutting or welding.

### What is the mandatory jobsite weather stopping threshold?
While European standard EN 40-3 governs column structural wind moments in permanent service, field erection operations must be immediately suspended if site wind gusts exceed 8.0 m/s (28.8 km/h) to protect rigging stability.
