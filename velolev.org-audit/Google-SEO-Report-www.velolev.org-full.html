1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive SEO audit of www.velolev.org for a B2B Industrial Equipment Manufacturer & Exporter (Portable Light Pole Lifter) site, generated on November 11, 2026. Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

84/100
SEO Health Score
Critical Issues Found:
  1. Brand Entity & Domain Mismatch: Domain is velolev.org but brand identity is exclusively volvet across all pages, titles, logos, and schemas without entity aliasing or sameAs linkage.
  2. Double Redirect Chain on Bare Domain: http://velolev.org -> 308 -> https://velolev.org/ -> 308 -> https://www.velolev.org/ (2 hops).
  3. Missing /sitemap.xml: Default /sitemap.xml returns HTTP 404; only /sitemap-index.xml is served, without a server redirect.
  4. Client-side Meta-Refresh (HTTP 200) on Legacy Routes: /equipment/ and /pricing-plan/ serve HTML 200 with meta-refresh rather than server-side HTTP 308 redirects.
  5. Unoptimized Heavy Image Payloads: Reference jobsite JPEG images on homepage reach 896 KB each (2.55 MB total for 3 thumbnails), lacking WebP/AVIF compression.
Quick Wins:
  1. Add 308 redirect rules in vercel.json for /sitemap.xml -> /sitemap-index.xml, /equipment/ -> /products/light-pole-jack/, and /pricing-plan/ -> /quote/.
  2. Compress reference images to WebP/AVIF (680x380 px) to save 2.4 MB (94% payload reduction) on the homepage.
  3. Shorten 37 titles (>65 chars) and 47 meta descriptions (>160 chars) to prevent Google SERP truncation.
  4. Add Content-Security-Policy (CSP) header in vercel.json.
  5. Fix H1 typography spacing glitches (e.g., 'Light pole lifting.Made portable.' -> 'Light pole lifting. Made portable.').

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 84/100

What Works

  • Fast Edge serving on Vercel with HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 support
  • TLS 1.3 encryption with valid Let's Encrypt certificate (88 days remaining)
  • HSTS enabled with strict preload requirements (max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains; preload)
  • Security headers: nosniff, SAMEORIGIN, strict-origin-when-cross-origin, Permissions-Policy
  • Clean canonical URLs across all 143 sitemap pages (100% self-referencing matches)
  • robots.txt explicitly permits all major search bots and AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, etc.)
  • Clean 404 handling without soft-404 traps

Findings

Double Redirect Chain on Bare HTTP Domain High

http://velolev.org performs a 308 redirect to https://velolev.org/, which then executes a second 308 redirect to https://www.velolev.org/. Double hops dilute link equity and waste crawl budget.

Recommendation: Update Vercel domain routing or middleware so http://velolev.org redirects directly to https://www.velolev.org/ in a single 308 hop.

Standard /sitemap.xml Returns HTTP 404 High

Many crawlers, third-party SEO auditing tools, and search bots query /sitemap.xml by default. Currently, https://www.velolev.org/sitemap.xml returns HTTP 404, while the valid index is at /sitemap-index.xml.

Recommendation: Add a permanent redirect rule in vercel.json from /sitemap.xml to /sitemap-index.xml.

Legacy Routes Use HTTP 200 Client Meta-Refresh Instead of Server Redirects High

Paths /equipment/ and /pricing-plan/ return HTTP 200 with <meta http-equiv='refresh'> and <meta name='robots' content='noindex'>. Search engines may delay deindexing and crawling of the true destination.

Recommendation: Configure permanent HTTP 308 redirects in vercel.json for /equipment/ -> /products/light-pole-jack/ and /pricing-plan/ -> /quote/.

Missing Content-Security-Policy (CSP) Header Low

The server does not transmit a Content-Security-Policy header, leaving an opening for potential script injection or framing risks.

Recommendation: Define an appropriate CSP header within vercel.json headers configuration.

Hardcoded Static lastmod Timestamps in Sitemap Low

All 143 entries in sitemap-0.xml carry the identical static date '2026-10-28T00:00:00.000Z', preventing search engines from recognizing true incremental article updates.

Recommendation: Dynamically derive lastmod in astro.config.mjs serialize hook using article publish/update metadata.

2.2 Content Quality & E-E-A-T

Score: 82/100

What Works

  • Exceptional engineering rigor: F001-F031 physical fact cards with clear distinction between confirmed specs and pending field validation
  • Explicit negative scope boundaries protecting customer safety (strict 12 m height and 300 kg load limits)
  • Comprehensive E-E-A-T institutional pages: About Us, Editorial Policy, Privacy Notice, Terms
  • High topical depth in English pillar articles (1,500 - 3,500 words with calculation formulas and crane cost comparisons)

Findings

Critical Brand Entity & Domain Disconnect (velolev.org vs volvet) Critical

The domain is velolev.org, yet the brand is exclusively identified as 'volvet' across all page titles, logos, schemas, and body copy. There is zero on-page or schema explanation of the relationship between velolev and volvet.

Recommendation: Add clear corporate brand entity linkage: explain on /about-us/ that volvet is the brand/trademark hosted at velolev.org, add sameAs and alternateName properties in Organization schema, or align domain branding.

Multilingual Asymmetry (35 English Pages vs 18 in Other Locales) Medium

English offers 35 pages including deep equipment configurations and comparison guides. In contrast, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Thai, and Indonesian only have 18 pages each, leaving 17 key articles unlocalized.

Recommendation: Translate the 5 configuration pages and high-performing guide articles into the 6 supported international target markets.

Thin Content Signals in Thai (/th/) Pages Low

14 pages in the Thai (/th/) subdirectory extract to under 250 words, partly due to lack of space delimiters in Thai and brief translated copy, posing a potential low-quality content risk in regional SERPs.

Recommendation: Enrich Thai technical guides with fuller localization and descriptive engineering vocabulary.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 85/100

What Works

  • 100% of 143 pages have title tags and meta descriptions
  • 100% of 143 pages follow the single H1 hierarchy standard
  • Consistent brand suffix appending (' | volvet') on all titles
  • Clean URL structure with trailing slashes and descriptive lowercase slugs
  • Zero orphan pages: all 143 sitemap pages are discoverable through internal links

Findings

37 Title Tags Exceed 65 Characters (SERP Truncation) Medium

37 pages carry titles between 66 and 82 characters (e.g. 'M16–M22 4-Bolt Portable Light Pole Lifter Configuration (180–260 mm Span) | volvet' - 82 chars), resulting in truncated display snippets in Google search results.

Recommendation: Trim titles to 55-60 characters while front-loading core target keywords (e.g., 'M16–M22 Pole Lifter Configuration (180–260mm) | volvet').

47 Meta Descriptions Exceed 160 Characters Medium

47 pages have descriptions ranging from 161 to 225 characters (e.g., /es/equipment/ at 225 chars), causing search engines to truncate sentences mid-phrase.

Recommendation: Condense descriptions to 140-155 characters with clear B2B procurement calls-to-action.

Punctuation and Typography Spacing Glitches in H1 Tags Low

Multiple H1 headings lack spacing after sentence-terminating periods, such as 'Light pole lifting.Made portable.' and 'Calculate a scenario.Keep the assumptions.'. Additionally, 'lightpole' is written as a single compound word on the equipment page.

Recommendation: Ensure proper spacing after periods ('Light pole lifting. Made portable.') and use standard terminology ('light pole').

2.4 Schema & Structured Data

Score: 88/100

What Works

  • 100% schema deployment across all 143 pages with 0 JSON syntax errors
  • Proper deployment of Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Product, TechArticle, and FAQPage
  • Rich PropertyValue specification cards embedded in Product schema

Findings

Product Schema Missing 'offers' and Product Identifiers Medium

Product schema on configuration pages omits the 'offers' property, triggering Google Rich Result warnings in Search Console. It also lacks 'sku' and 'mpn'.

Recommendation: Include an Offer object with availability ('https://schema.org/InStock') and priceSpecification or price on request, plus an MPN/SKU identifier.

Organization Schema Lacks sameAs Entity Links & Physical Address Low

Organization schema lists name, description, logo, and contactPoint, but lacks sameAs links to official social profiles, registries, and physical company address.

Recommendation: Add sameAs links and PostalAddress in Organization schema to solidify Google Knowledge Graph entity mapping.

TechArticle Schema Omits Explicit Author Object Low

Guide pages deploy TechArticle schema without an explicit Person or Organization author entity.

Recommendation: Add author: {'@type': 'Organization', 'name': 'volvet Technical Engineering Team'} to TechArticle schema.

2.5 Performance (Core Web Vitals)

Score: 78/100

What Works

  • Zero-JS / minimal-JS static architecture with zero framework client hydration overhead
  • Tiny external CSS payload (31.5 KB total across 2 files)
  • Web font (/fonts/geologica-latin.woff2 - 24.6 KB) preloaded with crossorigin
  • Above-the-fold hero image preloaded with fetchpriority='high'
  • Sub-150ms TTFB on Vercel Global Edge Network

Findings

Heavy Uncompressed Reference Images (2.55 MB on Homepage) High

Three reference jobsite photos on the homepage are served as uncompressed JPEGs: jobsite-rural-solar-street-light.jpg (897 KB), jobsite-parking-lot-tight-space.jpg (834 KB), jobsite-sidewalk-municipal-road.jpg (819 KB). Total image payload is 2.55 MB for 3 thumbnail cards.

Recommendation: Convert all content images to WebP/AVIF format and scale them to their display dimensions (680x380 px at 80% quality), reducing file size from 850 KB to ~35 KB per image (94% savings).

Hero Image Served as 196 KB PNG Without Modern Format Variants Medium

The hero cutout image (/images/product/portable-pole-lifter-hero-cutout.png) is a 195.7 KB PNG file without WebP/AVIF fallback or picture srcset element.

Recommendation: Provide WebP/AVIF version with alpha channel to cut hero payload down to ~45 KB.

2.6 AI Search & GEO Readiness

Score: 92/100

What Works

  • llms.txt (6.8 KB) and llms-full.txt (59.3 KB) deployed and fully accessible at domain root
  • robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Bytespider, and OAI-SearchBot
  • Fully server-rendered content (911 words on homepage) accessible without headless browser execution
  • Systematic F001-F031 physical fact cards providing grounded engineering data for LLM citation

Findings

llms.txt Formatting Hygiene Warnings Low

llms.txt includes parameter specifications under H2 headings rather than link lists [Title](URL) as proposed in llmstxt.org v2, producing 11 item-without-link parser notes.

Recommendation: Refactor llms.txt so general specifications precede the first H2, and H2 sections consist strictly of markdown links to published articles and data sheets.

Well-Known AI Discovery Files Absent Low

Probes to /.well-known/ai-catalog.json and /.well-known/agent-card.json return HTTP 404.

Recommendation: Optionally deploy ai-catalog.json and agent-card.json to advertise machine-readable endpoints.

2.7 Images

Score: 80/100

What Works

  • Descriptive, keyword-rich alt text on 100% of content photos
  • Explicit width and height HTML attributes preventing Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)
  • Native loading='lazy' attribute configured on all below-the-fold images

Findings

Tracking / Spacer 1x1 Base64 GIF Lacks Alt Tag Low

Base64 1x1 spacer GIF present across all pages lacks alt='' attribute or role='presentation', creating an accessibility/SEO audit warning.

Recommendation: Add alt='' or aria-hidden='true' to the spacer image element.
